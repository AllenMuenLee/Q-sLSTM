# QLSTM vs Q-sLSTM (odd gate) — scalar tasks

Sweep: `results/scalar_tasks/paper/2026-09-28`, 20 paired seeds per task, best-validation checkpoint.

Q-sLSTM here is the odd-gate recurrence: gates (1+q)/(1−q) of the VQC expectation q, xLSTM-stabilized. Both models have 45 trainable parameters.

**Reading the tables.** Values are mean over seeds (median in brackets). *ratio* = Q-sLSTM / QLSTM geometric mean with a paired-bootstrap 95% CI (< 1 = Q-sLSTM lower). *wins* = seeds where Q-sLSTM is better. *p* = Wilcoxon signed-rank, Holm-adjusted over the six tasks. *r* = rank-biserial effect, + = Q-sLSTM better.

Replay check: re-running each best checkpoint on the test set reproduces the recorded test MSE (max relative difference < 0.1%).
